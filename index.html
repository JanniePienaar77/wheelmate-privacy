Wheelmate. Nobody gets left behind.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

The short version. Wheelmate shares a rider's live location with the people on the same group ride, so nobody gets left behind. We collect only what that needs: your first name, the first letter of your surname, and your location while you are sharing it on a ride. Watchers do not share their location. We never sell your data, show ads or use analytics tools. Rides and all location points are deleted automatically within 30 days. You can delete your profile and ride history in the app at any time, or ask us to do it: delete your data.

1. Who we are

Wheelmate is a group-ride safety app made by Jannie Pienaar, an individual developer in South Africa. Jannie is the "responsible party" for your personal information under the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA), and its Information Officer.

Is it voluntary? Yes. But the app cannot work without a name, and riders cannot be tracked without location. If you don't give them, you can still watch a ride. No law requires us to collect this information.

Our legal basis. We process your information with your consent, which you give when you set up your profile and when you allow location access and tap Start sharing, and because it is needed to provide the service you asked for (POPIA section 11(1)(a) and (b)). You can withdraw consent at any time (section 6).

3. Who can see your information

  • People on the same ride (riders, leaders and watchers who have the ride code) see your display name, group, status and live position during the ride. Share ride codes only with people you trust.
  • Service providers that run the app for us, only to provide the service:
    • Fly.io: server hosting, United States
    • Neon: database hosting (on Amazon Web Services), United States
    • Expo (650 Industries): delivering push notifications to group leaders, and app updates. United States.
    • Apple: push notifications and maps on iPhone, and TestFlight during beta testing
    • Google: push notifications on Android (Firebase Cloud Messaging)
    • Cloudflare: hosting this website (its servers worldwide briefly process visitors' IP addresses to deliver the pages)
    • Tally BV: the beta sign-up form on our website. Stores sign-ups in the European Union; its notification emails to us are sent through a provider in the United States.
  • Google Maps on Android. The map on Android phones is provided by Google. Google's map software collects technical information such as your device type, IP address, a map-specific identifier and how you move the map, and uses it under Google's own privacy policy. It does not receive your ride or your name from us. On iPhone the map is Apple Maps.

We do not sell or rent your information, and we do not share it with anyone else unless the law requires us to.

4. Where your information is stored (outside South Africa)

Our server and database are in the United States, and beta sign-ups from our website are stored by Tally in the European Union, so your information is transferred outside South Africa. Under section 72 of POPIA we make this transfer because you consent to it when you set up your profile or sign up for the beta, and because it is needed to provide the service you asked for. Our providers protect the data under their own data protection terms. If you do not agree to this transfer, please do not set up a profile; you can still watch rides.

5. How long we keep it

  • Rides, ride membership and all location points: deleted automatically, at the latest 30 days after the ride was created. Nothing is kept longer. We keep them this long only so we can look into a missed or false alert if someone reports one.
  • Push tokens are stored with the ride membership and are deleted with it.
  • Beta sign-ups from the website: deleted when the beta ends or at most 12 months after you signed up, whichever comes first, or sooner if you ask us.
  • Data and deletion requests: we keep a record of the request (your email address, what you asked and when we did it) for 12 months after we close it, so we can show we handled it. It contains no location data.
  • Your profile (name, initial, nickname, profile ID): kept until you delete it. If you uninstall the app without deleting it, use our deletion page.

6. Your rights and choices

  • Stop sharing location: tap Stop sharing or Leave ride, or turn off Location for Wheelmate in your phone's Settings. This withdraws your consent for location; it does not affect what was collected before.
  • Delete your data in the app: Profile → Delete my profile. This deletes your profile and your ride history, including all your location points, from our server straight away.
  • Delete your data without the app: see delete your data. We do it within 7 days and confirm by email.
  • Access or correct: edit your name in the app, or email us to ask what we hold about you.
  • Object: you may object to the processing at any time by emailing us (POPIA section 11(3)).
  • Complain: please email us first. You also have the right to complain to South Africa's Information Regulator: inforegulator.org.za, POPIAComplaints@inforegulator.org.za, Woodmead North Office Park, 54 Maxwell Drive, Woodmead, Johannesburg, 2191.

7. Security

All traffic between the app and our server is encrypted (HTTPS). The server only accepts requests from the Wheelmate app, leader rights need a separate leader code, and only the developer can access the database. If a security breach affects your personal information, we will tell you and the Information Regulator as POPIA section 22 requires.

8. Children

Wheelmate is meant for adults (18 and over). Younger riders may only use it with their parent or guardian's permission, and the parent or guardian is then the person who consents.

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. If a change affects what we collect or how we use it, we will tell you in the app and ask for your consent again first.
