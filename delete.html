Delete your Wheelmate data

Wheelmate by Jannie Pienaar · Last updated: 3 November 2026

Request deletion by email: send an email to janniepienaar@gmail.com with the subject Delete my data. You don't need to install the app again.

Include your profile reference (Profile screen in the app, 8 characters) if you have it. If you don't, give the first name and surname initial you used, and the date and ride code of a ride you joined, so we can find the right profile.

Delete in the app (immediate)

  1. Open Wheelmate and tap your profile.
  2. Tap Delete my profile and confirm.

This deletes everything at once and cannot be undone.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile: first name, surname initial, nickname and profile ID.
  • All your ride memberships: group, leader status, ride status and push token.
  • All your location points.

We do this within 7 days of your email and reply to confirm when it's done.

What is kept, and for how long

  • If you created a ride, the ride's name and organiser name stay with that ride until it is deleted automatically, at the latest 30 days after it was created. Ask us in your email if you want it removed sooner.
  • Nothing else is kept. All rides and location points are deleted automatically within 30 days anyway.

More detail is in our privacy policy.
